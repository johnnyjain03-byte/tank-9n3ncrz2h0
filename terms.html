Gym Bro health & safety

Please read this before you train with Gym Bro.

Not medical advice

Gym Bro gives general exercise, food and lifestyle suggestions. It doesn't diagnose, treat or replace a doctor, physiotherapist, dietitian or other health professional, and it can't examine you.

Check with a professional first

Talk to your GP, specialist or midwife before you start, or before you change how you train, if any of these apply:

  • a heart condition, chest pain, high or low blood pressure, diabetes, asthma or another long-term condition
  • dizziness or fainting, or a doctor has told you to exercise only under supervision
  • you take regular medicine
  • an injury, operation, or bone, joint or muscle problem
  • you're pregnant or have had a baby in the last year
  • you haven't exercised for a long time, or you're unsure whether it's safe for you

The health check in the app helps Gym Bro be more careful with you, but it can't replace that conversation.

Stop if something's wrong

Stop straight away if you get chest pain or tightness, feel faint or dizzy, are much more out of breath than usual, or feel sharp or unusual pain. Chest pain that doesn't go away is an emergency: call 999 (112 in the EU, 911 in the US). For other pain that lasts, see your GP or a physiotherapist.

You're in charge

You decide what you do. Use weights you can control, with good form, and adjust to how you feel on the day. Gym Bro's numbers are starting points, not targets you must hit.

Responsibility

Gym Bro is offered as it is. We aren't responsible for injury, illness or other harm that comes from following its suggestions, except where the law doesn't allow us to limit our responsibility, for example for injury caused by our own negligence. Nothing here affects your legal rights.

Your health information

What you tell Gym Bro about your health and injuries stays on your phone. The only exception is if you ask for AI tips about something you typed yourself: then just those words are sent for that one answer. See the privacy page.

AI tips can be wrong. Gym Bro only uses them to be more careful with you, never to say an exercise is safe.
