Gym Bro privacy

Gym Bro is a personal training and food planner. This page explains what it stores and where.

On your phone

Your profile, logs, meals, weights and photos are stored only on your phone. Photos leave it only when you ask Gym Bro to analyse one (see below). You can back up and delete everything from the app.

Health and injuries: the conditions, injuries and answers you give in the health check are stored only on your phone. They're never sent to the Gym Bro server or to the photo analysis, with one exception you choose: if you type in "Something else" and tap "Get AI tips", only the words you typed are passed through the Gym Bro server to Claude (Anthropic) for that one answer. Nothing else about you goes with them, and neither keeps them. The answers are included in a backup file if you save one, so keep that file private.

On the Gym Bro server

  • Notifications: if you turn them on, your notification subscription, your reminder times and which of today's tasks are done, so reminders skip what you've already done.
  • Rest timer: with notifications on, when you rest between sets in gym mode, when the rest ends and what your next set is (for example "Leg press, set 3 of 3: 10–15 reps at 140 kg"), so it can send the "10 seconds left" notification. It's deleted as soon as that's sent.
  • Calendar: your next two weeks of sessions (dates, times, titles), so they can appear in your calendar.
  • Apple Health (optional): if you set up the Shortcut, daily steps, active calories, resting heart rate, weight and the times and titles of your next week's calendar events. Deleted after 35 days.

Food lookups

When you scan a barcode, the camera picture stays on your phone and only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, a free food database, to find the calories. Searching for packaged food sends your search words there too.

Photo analysis (optional)

When you tap to analyse a meal photo, estimate body fat from photos or run a physique check, a smaller copy of the photo (about 1024 pixels) is sent to the Gym Bro server and passed straight to Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, together with what the analysis needs: your sex, age, height, weight, waist and goal for body photos, or the description you typed for a meal. The Gym Bro server doesn't store or log the photo. Anthropic handles it under its commercial terms and privacy policy. Only the result (calories, a body fat range, suggested focus areas) is saved, on your phone. The server counts how many analyses each phone asks for per day, to keep the costs down.

Ask Gym Bro (optional)

When you send a question in the chat, it goes through the Gym Bro server to Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, together with the last few messages and a short summary of your plan: sex, age, height, weight, goal, daily calorie and protein targets, today's session and what you've eaten so far today. Your health and injury answers go along only if you tick "Use my health answers". The Gym Bro server doesn't store the conversation; it only counts how many questions each phone asks per day, to keep the costs down. The chat history is kept on your phone and you can clear it.

Weather (optional)

If you set your town, Gym Bro keeps its location rounded to about 10 km and asks Open-Meteo, a free weather service, for the forecast. With notifications on, the rounded location is also stored on the Gym Bro server so reminders can mention rain or cold. Turn it off in the app and it's deleted at the next sync.

Google Calendar (optional)

If you connect Google Calendar, Gym Bro asks only for permission to create and manage its own "Gym Bro" calendar. It can't see or change your other calendars or events. It writes your sessions into that calendar and reads back changes you make to them there. The access token is stored on the Gym Bro server. Disconnecting in the app deletes the Gym Bro calendar and revokes access; you can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Gym Bro's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is never sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.
